---
name: usage-bar
description: Inspect provider quota headroom shown in the Usage Bar above the BB footer.
---

Use `bb usage-bar` for provider quotas, `bb usage-bar --all --force` to refresh all reporting hosts, or `bb usage-bar --tokens` for recorded totals. Click the sidebar bar for details. The plugin works without Usage Limits. Counts are not billing data and exclude history BB never recorded.
